Jellyfin Discovery — HSS Shelves

These are server-wide HSS fallback shelves. The browser extension has separate per-user Home, Movies and Shows profiles under Customize; changes here do not overwrite those profiles.

A configurable number of themed shelves rotates daily. Seasonal shelves appear in their scheduled months, in addition to the daily shelves. Empty shelves stay hidden.

Default: UTC. Controls the daily change and month boundaries.
Enabled turns a shelf on/off. Months: [] means all year; [10] means October; [12] means December. Types: Movie and/or Series. GenresAny and TagsAny match exact names, ignoring case; when both are provided both filters must match. MinRating and MaxMinutes are optional. Keep Key unchanged to preserve its Home Screen Sections settings. After changing titles or adding/removing rules, restart Jellyfin. Filter/schedule changes apply on the next home reload. Keep all desired rotating rows enabled in HSS and choose which participate here.
